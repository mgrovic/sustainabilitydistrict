    About the DEI Business Fellows Program

    Our Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Fellows Program empowers underrepresented entrepreneurs and small business owners in the travel and tourism sector. Through this initiative, fellows receive:

    • Complementary marketing support
    • Mentorship from industry leaders
    • Educational resources and workshops
    • Networking opportunities to expand their reach

    The program fosters a more inclusive and equitable industry, providing businesses the tools and support needed to thrive.

    ✦ Understanding Our GDS-Index Data

    The Global Destination Sustainability Index provides a comprehensive assessment of our destination's sustainability performance across multiple categories.

    Our metrics are divided into four main categories: Supplier, Destination Management Performance, Social, and Environmental. Each category contributes to our overall score and helps identify areas for improvement.

    Environmental

    Includes carbon footprint and renewable energy initiatives

    Social

    Covers diversity and community engagement

    Supplier

    Evaluates local suppliers and sustainable sourcing

    Destination Management

    Strategies and policies that support local sustainability
